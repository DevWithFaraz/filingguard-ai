## Opening this vault

In Obsidian, choose **Open folder as vault** and select
`C:\Users\IT\Documents\FilingGuard AI`, then open this note. Do not create a
second nested vault. Configuration is under `.obsidian`; only core plugins are
enabled, with no community plugin dependency.

Use backlinks or Local Graph on a feature/decision note to explore its connections.
The links help retrieval; they do not guarantee automatic AI recall. Begin future
work with the entry notes above and the specific context needed for that task.

Setup follows [Obsidian's existing-folder workflow](https://obsidian.md/help/vault).
See [[docs/knowledge/Documentation Workflow#Vault boundaries|Vault boundaries]]
before enabling any syncing or publishing service.
