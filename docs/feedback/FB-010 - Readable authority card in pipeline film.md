# Readable authority card in pipeline film

## Source and need
After viewing the rendered desktop film, the user supplied an Authority-lookup
frame and said the text in the white card had decreased too much, was not visible
and looked bad. This is direct aesthetic/readability feedback on the real output.

## Response and validation
The first revision scaled an 820px document into a 330px card, making names and
citations too small. The October 7 source revision replaces that inner content with
a focused list: native 32px case names and 24px citations, with connectors sharing
the row centers. The persistent filing container and beat timing are retained.
Source typecheck passes; new stills and 44 decoded frames confirm the larger
anchors and clear transitions. Desktop/mobile continuous browser loops and
homepage checks pass. User aesthetic acceptance remains separate.
The earlier pause is historical; the user resumed and broadened the revision in
[[docs/feedback/FB-011 - Richer programmatic film direction]]. Previous exports are
preserved in `apps/video/out/revisions/v1`.
