# FB-015 - Filing-centric product direction and EU AI Act assessment

## Source
User, 2026-10-14. Paraphrase: revisit FilingGuard, court rules and the EU AI Act;
keep the product practical for public use, use official sources, avoid unsupported
coverage or compliance claims, and make the smallest safe first improvement.

## Need and impact
The observed need is a clear product boundary and a credible path from a working
technical MVP to a real pre-filing product. The proposed Article 50 idea and broad
court coverage are potential directions, not proof of a user demand or a mandate to
build every one now.

## Response
Accepted the filing-centric direction and a reference-only court-rule source
foundation. Article 50 remains a separate future direction rather than a court
profile. See [[docs/decisions/ADR-014 - Filing-centric court rules and separate Article 50 direction]]
and [[TODO]] T111/T106.

## Validation
Before activating a real court profile, validate demand for one filing context,
current applicability with official sources and complete requirement coverage.
Before creating an Article 50 module, validate a distinct provider/deployer user
workflow and a scoped evaluation set. Do not infer either from this request alone.
