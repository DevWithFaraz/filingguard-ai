## Need and impact
The first rendered film's overall visual treatment and background did not meet
the user's desired richness. Passing export metadata did not establish aesthetic
acceptance. The earlier nine-frame reference remains visual inspiration.

## Response

Author a lit evidence workbench: structured filing contours and a perspective
floor, layered paper, beveled glass source cards, precise traveling evidence
paths, meaningful scan/highlight movements and a deeper brand reveal. Essential
evidence text remains large and settles for reading. No new legal engines or
provider calls are introduced. The rejected overlay phrases remain omitted.

Because the shared background changes across the whole film, rerender both
compositions after still review. Preserve the first exports in ignored revision
storage. No commit, push or deployment is authorized.

critique scores are 8–9; continuous desktop/mobile loops and22 browser cases plus

pass. [[apps/video/brief/REVIEW]] records brisk reading windows and minor WebM
grain smoothing. User aesthetic acceptance remains separate from these results.
