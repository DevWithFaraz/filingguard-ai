# Brand bible - pipeline film

Owner of brand values used by `src/brand.ts`. Palette and tone come from
[[docs/product/UI direction]] and [[design]]; do not invent new brand colors.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ink | `#0E0620` | deepest background |
| night | `#170A33` | background body |
| purple | `#3E0F8D` | brand accent, glows |
| logoPurple | `#2D0A6A` | logo-matched dark fill |
| lilac | `#9564DD` | citation highlights, edges, focus |
| yellow | `#E4DA72` | selected emphasis / logo nodes only - never success or severity |
| paper | `#F4F0FB` | document sheet, cards (light) |
| gray | `#EEEEEE` | neutral surfaces |
| charcoal | `#24292B` | text on paper |

Status colors always pair with an icon and a text label:
resolved `#3DBE8B` + check, review/critical `#F0726B` + "!", partial/altered
`#F2A65A` + "~", unavailable/unresolved `#9A94AE` + "?".

## Type
Inter (OFL) 500–750 for headings and UI; JetBrains Mono (OFL) for citations,
offsets and identifiers. GC Avalance is NOT used in the film until the user
confirms its permission covers video distribution. Desktop headline 80px,
support 30px, eyebrow 23px mono uppercase tracked 0.13em. Authority anchors use
native 32px case labels and 24px citations inside the 330px sheet. No negative
letter-spacing.

## Logo
The original `filingguard-logo.png` (transparent, 2172x724) is shown unchanged on a
light frosted card, matching the site header treatment. Its three yellow node
centers (pixel space: 458,346 / 313,442 / 405,490) are animation targets.
Never recolor, crop or distort the logo.

## Texture
October 7 treatment: lit filing contours, a perspective workbench floor, bundled
evidence paths, subtle static grain and a readability vignette. Beveled glass,
layered paper and narrow luminous edges give the panels depth. See
[[docs/feedback/FB-011 - Richer programmatic film direction]].
Banned: centered title on a plain gradient, gradient orbs, particle clouds,
spinning gavels/scales, fake 3D server racks, lens flares.
